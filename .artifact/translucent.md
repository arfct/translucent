Translucent is a visionOS app that opens websites as windows in a room. A window can hold a whole site, such as Reddit or Discord, or a single-purpose page such as a clock, a timer or an orrery. Each window has its own size, a glass, transparent or opaque background, and optional custom fonts and CSS. Favorites appear in the launcher grid. The directory at translucent.directory lists example windows under Examples, Websites, Widgets and Experimental.

The first image shows the launcher with nine favorites, beside a New York Times front page with its window menu open. The second shows the same room from further back, with the orrery on the wall to the left.

The first commit is from January 2024. Translucent was released on the App Store on March 21, 2024, and the current version is 1.0.3. The app is free.

Swift, SwiftUI, WebKit. 2024.
